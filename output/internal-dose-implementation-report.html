01

The gap

The report's canonical structure (NIEHS Report 10) includes an Internal Dose Assessment section in Results, holding a Summary of Plasma Concentration Data table and a toxicokinetic narrative. In the current pipeline that section renders as an empty placeholder — "Narrative pending" — while the underlying plasma data is instead surfaced as a generic apical endpoint table labelled "Tissue Concentration."

Today

Plasma data treated as one more apical platform. A routine dose-response table, no kinetic interpretation. The dedicated Internal Dose section stays blank.

Proposed

The same plasma data routed to the Internal Dose section, with computed half-lives, a dose-proportionality read, and a narrative that interprets them — matching the reference report.

This is not missing data or missing processing. The data is present and correctly parsed. What's missing is the analysis that turns plasma concentrations into an internal-dose assessment, and the routing that puts it in the right section.

02

Why the section depends on the plasma data

"Internal dose" is the exposure that actually reaches the bloodstream, as distinct from the dose administered. The reference report's assessment is built entirely on measured plasma concentrations of PFHxSAm, sampled at two timepoints after the final dose:

  • 2 hours and 24 hours postdose, on study day 4.
  • In the 4 and 37 mg/kg groups, both sexes (3 dedicated animals per sex per group).
  • Quantified by UPLC-MS/MS; limit of quantitation 10.0 ng/mL; all study values above the LOQ.

Every interpretive claim in the reference narrative is derived from those two numbers per group: the half-life (from the decline between 2 h and 24 h), the dose proportionality (whether concentration scales with dose), and the resulting ADME inference. Without the plasma data there is, literally, nothing for the section to say — which is exactly why the template binds it to the plasma/tissue-concentration platform.

03

The method

Half-life is estimated as first-order elimination between the two measured timepoints. From the group-mean concentrations at 2 h and 24 h:

ke = ln( C2h / C24h )t24h − t2h (h−1)
t½ = ln 2ke = 0.693ke (h)

The interval t24h − t2h is 22 hours. Two supporting reads follow directly from the same concentrations:

  • Dose proportionality — compare the concentration ratio (37 mg/kg ÷ 4 mg/kg) against the dose ratio (9.25×). Sub-proportional scaling signals altered absorption or induced clearance.
  • Bioaccumulation screen — a screening-level flag when t½ > 24 h, per the reference's stated intent.
  • Below-LOQ handling — substitute ½ · LOD when >20% of a group is above the limit of detection (reference footnote). Not triggered here — all values are above the LOQ — but implemented for generality.

05

How we implement it

Four pieces, scoped small. The numeric work is deterministic (byte-verifiable against the reference); only the prose is generated.

  1. deterministic

    Toxicokinetic analysis

    A module that reads the plasma-concentration data already in the pool and computes, per (sex, dose): mean ± SEM at each timepoint, elimination rate ke, half-life t½, the dose-proportionality ratio, and the bioaccumulation flag. Pure computation — no LLM.

  2. deterministic

    Plasma concentration table

    Feed the computed summary into the Summary of Plasma Concentration Data table the template already declares — reproducing Table 7.

  3. generated

    Internal-dose narrative

    Prose grounded in the computed numbers, following the reference's interpretive frame: sex difference in exposure, sub-proportional dose scaling → altered ADME, shorter half-life at the higher dose → induced clearance. Cites the figures it stands on.

  4. routing

    Section wiring

    Route the computed table and narrative to the Internal Dose Assessment node in the document tree, so the plasma data lands in its proper section instead of surfacing as a generic apical platform.

06

Decisions for you

Points where we want your sign-off before building. Our recommendation is given; the reference report supports each.

Half-life from group means

Compute t½ from the group-mean concentrations (not per-animal then averaged). This is what reproduces the published values exactly. Recommended.

Below-limit substitution

Use ½ · LOD when >20% of a group is above the limit of detection, per the Table 7 footnote. Inert for this study (all above LOQ). Confirm rule.

Bioaccumulation flag

Surface the screening call when t½ > 24 h. Here it flags the 4 mg/kg groups (78.2 h, 40.1 h). Recommended.

Interpretive claims

The ADME / clearance-induction language is generated, then reviewed and edited externally like every other narrative — never auto-published as final. Per the round-trip model.
