# NIEHS-13: NIEHS Report on the In Vivo Repeat Dose Biological Potency Study of 1,4-Dichlorobenzene (CASRN 106-46-7) in Sprague Dawley (Hsd:Sprague Dawley® SD®) Rats and B6D2F1/Crl Mice via Whole Body Respiratory Exposure

Source: https://cebs.niehs.nih.gov/cebs/data/publication/NIEHS-13 (DOI 10.22427/NIEHS-DATA-NIEHS-13)
Downloaded 2026-10-01: 142 of 142 listed files

## Key facts

- 1,4-Dichlorobenzene (1,4-DCB), studies 108020004 (rats) / 108020005 (mice): female Sprague Dawley rats (0–800 ppm, 7 groups) and B6D2F1/Crl mice (0–400 ppm, 6 groups), whole-body inhalation; heart, kidney, liver, lung, ovary. Companion to NIEHS-12 with the same layout and methods (Materials and Methods documents curve-fit BMR 2 SD; BMDExpress 3.20.0119).
- Same artefact set as NIEHS-12: per-species genomic and non-genomic .bm2 (+ JSON), raw counts, manifests, eFDR synthetic null, QC reports, removal workbooks, study tables, PDF report tables. The Analysis Pipeline Evaluation zips are byte-identical to NIEHS-12's.

## Package issues

- The rat raw-read-count archive is published as 'Attenuation-adjusted_Raw_Read_Counts_Rat_Study,zip' (comma, no extension).
